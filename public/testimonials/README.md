# Testimonial images

Drop testimonial screenshots and client photos/logos here, then reference them
in `lib/testimonials.ts`.

- **screenshot** — a picture of the real testimonial (WhatsApp chat, email, review).
  Example: `/testimonials/abc-logistics-chat.png`
- **image** — the person's photo or the company logo, used for the small avatar.
  Example: `/testimonials/abc-logistics-logo.png`

Recommended: PNG or JPG, under ~500KB each. Portrait screenshots look best.
